Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Overview

Silent Hello is designed to help users send a quiet daily presence signal to trusted contacts. We collect only the information needed to provide core app functionality.

Firebase services

Silent Hello uses Firebase services (such as authentication, cloud-based data storage, analytics, and crash reporting) to operate and improve reliability.

No location tracking

Silent Hello does not track your precise location for app functionality and does not use background location tracking.

Data access, export, and deletion

You can request data export and account deletion directly inside the app settings. These controls are available in-app so you can manage your information without contacting support.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@silenthello.app.
