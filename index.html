How Silent Hello works

Silent Hello is built around one simple idea: a gentle, daily signal that says, "I'm here." Open the app, send your check-in, and your selected contacts can see your daily presence status.

It is intentionally quiet and minimal, designed for low-pressure connection without constant messaging.
