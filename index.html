How Silent Hello works

Silent Hello is built around one simple idea: a gentle daily presence signal that says, “I’m here.”

  1. You tap a signal. OK, not my best day, or thinking of you.
  2. Your chosen people receive it. A calm notification — no pressure to reply.
  3. Connection stays easy. Quiet care, without constant messaging.
